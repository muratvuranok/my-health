# Product vision

**My Health — Your Family Health Operating System** (working positioning).

## Initial job to be done
An adult coordinating their own or a relative's complex medication and health routine needs one trustworthy place for schedules, measurements and medical-document history.

## Target segment hypothesis
Adults managing multiple medications and regular measurements; secondary segment is family caregivers. Validate with interviews rather than assuming market demand.

## Positioning
Do not build a feature-for-feature clone of Apple Health, Medisafe or Guava. Differentiate via safe daily workflow, permissioned family coordination, interoperability and local language/market fit.

## Markets and language
Türkiye and Azerbaijan first; Turkish, Azerbaijani and English UI; global-ready time zones, units, country configuration and pricing.

## Outcomes
Reduced missed tracking tasks, higher user confidence and repeat use. Do not claim clinical outcome improvement without evidence.

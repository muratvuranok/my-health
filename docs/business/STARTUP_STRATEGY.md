# Startup strategy — hypotheses and experiments

## Investment thesis
Become the trusted personal/family health coordination layer across medication, measurements and records. Not merely a prettier HealthKit dashboard.

## Validation sequence
1. Interview 30–40 prospective users across Türkiye/Azerbaijan; identify repetitive painful workflows.
2. Observe 10 prototype usability sessions using synthetic data.
3. Recruit consented private alpha cohort after safety gates.
4. Test subscription willingness to pay before committing to price.
5. Measure repeat usage, 30/90-day retention, medication workflow completion, family invitations, paid conversion, churn, CAC and gross margin.

## Pricing hypotheses (not approved prices)
Free basic tracking; Personal Plus around USD 4.99/month; Family around USD 9.99/month, localized and tested. Do not paywall core safety reminders.

## Distribution
Direct-to-consumer initially, referrals/family invitations; evaluate clinics, pharmacies, employers and insurers as future B2B2C channels. No sale of personal health data.

## Investor readiness
Document customer interviews, cohort definitions, privacy/legal review, security assessment, unit economics, revenue and investor data room. No invented traction or clinical claims.

## Competition and differentiation (E2, E3)
Evaluate Apple Health Medications (availability since iOS 16 to be verified), MyTherapy, Medisafe and caregiver workflows. Test caregiver-managed profiles independently from future linked-account grants; ADR-001 remains accepted.

## Commercial experiments (E6, E8, G5)
Prices USD 4.99/9.99 remain hypotheses: research local purchasing power, yearly/family prices and keep safety reminders free. Investigate pharmacies and chronic-condition groups; e-Nabız public third-party API status **to be verified**.
Proposed activation: at least 1 medication plan and 5 dose actions in the first 7 days; validate before adopting.
